Sign-ups

With sign-ups on, anyone who can reach Memos can make an account for themselves. Off, only an administrator adds people, here or in Memos.

Add someone

The password is made for them and shown once. They can change it in Memos → Settings → My Account.

Photos, documents and recordings attached to memos are kept as ordinary files in the memos shared folder, in attachments/, named with the time they were added. Leave them where they are — Memos finds each one by its name. To remove a file, remove it from its memo in Memos.

Memos needs no app from a store: its web app goes on a phone's home screen and opens like an app, ready to jot something down. Sign in with your Memos username and password.

Open Memos on a phone

  1. Scan the code with the phone's camera and sign in.
  2. iPhone and iPad, in Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Android, in Chrome: ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
